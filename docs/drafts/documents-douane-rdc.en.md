# Customs documents for a shipment to the DRC: the list to prepare

Smooth clearance starts with complete, accurate paperwork. Here is what to
prepare for a shipment to the Democratic Republic of the Congo, and what each
document is for.

> **Language rule.** The OCC and the DGDA require **all documents to be in
> French**. [CONFIRMED — trade.gov]

## The basic documents

1. **The commercial invoice.** It describes the goods, their quantity, unit and
   total value, and the **currency**. Stating the **HS code** (see below) helps
   classify the items correctly. For goods that are not sold (gift, sample), a
   **pro forma invoice** is used. [CONFIRMED — trade.gov]
2. **The packing list.** It details the contents of each parcel: number of
   parcels, weight, dimensions, nature of the items. It enables the physical
   check on arrival.
3. **The transport document**: bill of lading (sea) or air waybill (AWB) for
   air. [CONFIRMED — trade.gov]
4. **The certificate of origin** (often required), the **import licence**, the
   **insurance certificate** and the country-of-departure **export
   declaration**. [CONFIRMED — trade.gov]

## The HS code (Harmonised System)

The **Harmonised System** is the World Customs Organization's international
nomenclature for classifying goods, used by most countries. The first six digits
are common internationally; countries add sub-headings. The HS code determines
the tariff category and therefore the duties due. [CONFIRMED — WCO principle]

## Pre-shipment inspection (≥ USD 2,500)

For an import valued at **USD 2,500 or more**, a **BIVAC** inspection is carried
out in the country of departure; it checks that the quality, quantity and value
declared on the pro forma invoice match. The resulting **Verification Notice
(AV)** is required for clearance. [CONFIRMED — trade.gov] [TO VERIFY — threshold
at publication date]

## CN22 / CN23: for postal items

The **CN22** and **CN23** forms are the **Universal Postal Union (UPU)** customs
declarations, used for **postal items**. CN22 is for small low-value items, CN23
for larger ones. [CONFIRMED — UPU principle] [TO VERIFY — their use for a

## Two concrete situations

- **A parcel of clothes for family.** Pro forma invoice (real value, even for a
  gift), packing list, transport document. Under USD 2,500, no pre-shipment
  inspection. [TO VERIFY — minimum documents for an individual's shipment]
- **A batch of goods for a business.** Detailed commercial invoice with HS codes,
  packing list, certificate of origin, BIVAC inspection and Verification Notice,
  then declaration on arrival.
